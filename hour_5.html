Product Marketing & Store Build

Where stores leak money or stop it — product pages, photography, payments, and the platform the store actually runs on.

Shopify Build Ad Platform Setup
The Issue
This is where most stores leak money

Bad photos, feature-led copy ("100% cotton, 200gsm") instead of benefit-led ("the t-shirt that doesn't pill after three washes"), broken checkout, no clear shipping or returns info. Customers bounce in seconds if any of this feels off. Getting the basics right on photos, copy, and a working checkout puts you ahead of most launches. For a service, the "product page" is your offer page and "checkout" is recurring billing — Stripe Billing, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, or Memberstack handle it.

Keys to Success
  • Benefit-led copy, not feature-led. Lead with what the product does for the customer, not its specs.
  • Test the checkout end to end before any traffic — on desktop and mobile, with a real card.
  • Shipping and returns info visible on every product page. Missing it is a top bounce reason.
CoWork Ops Layer · Hour 5
Once the store and checkout are live, schedule this
Cadence: Daily · Trigger: Failed checkouts, abandoned carts
CoWork can do
  • Pull a daily report of failed checkouts and abandoned carts
  • Track conversion rate by product-page variant
  • Flag product pages missing photos, shipping info, or returns info
  • Reconcile payment-processor payouts against store-reported revenue
CoWork can't
  • Rewrite product copy or approve photography
  • Change payment-processor or checkout settings
  • Approve a real test purchase
Setup cost: ~1 hour once. Daily human time: ~10 minutes to review the report.
