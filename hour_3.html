The Issue
Sourcing shape depends on the model picked in Hour 1

The shape of "sourcing" depends entirely on the model picked in Hour 1:

  1. Physical product — print-on-demand, dropshipping, wholesale, or custom manufacturing (covered below)
  2. Service business — booking front-end, productizing the offer, provider sourcing
  3. App / software — visual no-code builder, AI prompt-to-app, or engineering. See the App Build Paths page for the full route.

Keys to Success
  • For products: order samples. Don't dropship a product you have not seen in person.
  • For services: productize from day one. Write the pricing page like you're selling a professional product, not your time.
  • For apps: pick your tier consciously. No-code trades speed for durability; AI prompt-to-app trades durability for speed. Pick based on the importance of speed to market.
  • Lock in unit economics, understand at what volume levels they start to change.
CoWork Ops Layer · Hour 3
Default loop · Physical productCadence: Weekly + event-driven · Trigger: Inventory thresholds, supplier emails
CoWork can
  • Monitor supplier inboxes for price changes, MOQ updates, lead-time changes
  • Track sample arrival vs. promised dates; build a vendor reliability scorecard
  • Compare landed cost across multiple suppliers in a running Excel sheet
  • Draft re-order POs from a template when inventory drops below threshold
  • Pull Alibaba/Faire/Spocket new-listing emails into a "potential sources" doc
Setup cost: ~1 hour once. Weekly human time: ~20 minutes.
