Formation, Banking & Finance Setup

The Issue

Most first-time founders waste their entire first day on entity formation, EIN application, and bank-account opening. The EIN alone can take an hour through the IRS website on a bad day, and bank account opening usually requires an in-person or video-verification step. We start Hour 1 with a real entity, a real bank account, and a real card — so the 8-hour clock is spent on the things that actually move the business: finding the product, building the store, running ads. This page is the home for every formation, legal-setup, and accounting decision.

CoWork Ops Layer · Hour 0

Cadence: weekly, Monday morning. Once Stripe, Shopify, and the bank/credit card are connected, schedule Claude CoWork to run a weekly money pass: pull Shopify's revenue/refund/fees report, pull the bank transaction export and credit card statement, reconcile against QuickBooks (or the Excel ledger), flag duplicate charges or forgotten subscriptions, and email the team a Monday-morning cash position with runway in weeks. It also flags any ad-platform spend over the daily cap, runs a weekly P&L pull with variance vs. plan, does a monthly sales-tax-nexus check per state, and posts a quarterly state-filing checklist. CoWork can't open or close accounts, authorize transfers, file a return, or judge whether a charge is legitimate — those stay with a human. Setup cost: ~45 minutes once. Ongoing: ~15 minutes per week to read the email and review the P&L.
