The Issue
Two channels, two kinds of creative

Most work happens after launch, but the infrastructure has to be live before the first ad dollar — and you want to understand the playing field before you launch. There are two distinct channels:

  • Organic content — free posts on your social, algorithm-distributed
  • Paid ads — you pay Meta, TikTok, Google, or Pinterest to put creative in front of specific people. Different goals, different creative.

Most brands run both: paid drives sales now, organic builds an audience for retargeting. Service marketing leans on Google Search, SEO, LinkedIn, and case studies over scroll-stopping creative.

Klaviyo and Google Analytics 4 must be live before any traffic — you cannot recover events that weren't tracked. The AI content saturation trap: the brands winning aren't posting the most AI content, they're posting AI content with a real human point of view layered on top. Use these tools to remove busywork, not to replace having something to say. AI allows much more content creation, but use it thoughtfully.

Keys to Success
  • Set daily ad caps before campaigns go live. $50–100/day per platform for week one. Test & analyze.
  • Three creatives per ad set, minimum. One winner, two to compare against. Need a baseline for testing.
  • Email flows live before launch: welcome (1 email), abandoned cart (3 emails), post-purchase (2 emails). Klaviyo has templates.
  • Human points of view added to AI content creation. No exceptions.
CoWork Ops Layer · Hour 6
Once campaigns and tracking are liveCadence: Daily (paid) + Weekly (organic) · Trigger: Spend pacing, ROAS thresholds
CoWork can
  • Daily pull of spend, ROAS, and CPA per platform into a single dashboard
  • Flag campaigns where Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) exceeds target by more than 25%
  • Reconcile platform-reported revenue against Shopify-reported revenue
  • Draft a weekly performance summary for the team
  • Generate UTM-tagged links for new campaigns from a brief
  • Pull Klaviyo email metrics and surface the winners weekly
  • Surface top-performing organic posts each week for paid promotion
Setup cost: ~2 hours once. Daily human time: ~15 minutes to review the dashboard.
