04

Brand Messaging

Name (not too complex or hard to say), easy to find in a URL, own the domain, a one-line brand message, and visuals that look intentional — enough brand to launch, it does not have to be perfect, it can evolve in the first few months.

The Issue
Don't optimize before there's data

Founders waste hours on logos and color palettes. The brand at launch needs four things: a name people can spell, a domain you own, a one-line value prop, and visuals that look intentional. You will redesign everything within six months once you know who actually buys — so don't optimize before there's data.

Keys to Success
  • Buy the domain at Cloudflare or Porkbun — not the platform's upsell. Saves $40–80/year and keeps DNS portable.
  • Don't get stuck on the logo. Looka, Canva, or Gemini for now — later on we can evolve to using consultants.
  • One-line value prop, written down. Every page, ad, and email pulls from this line.
CoWork Ops Layer · Hour 4
Once the domain and brand are live, schedule thisCadence: Weekly + event-driven · Trigger: Recurring + domain/DNS/SSL alerts
CoWork can do
  • Monitor site uptime and email the team on outages
  • Track domain renewal dates and DNS/SSL expiry — flag 30 days out
  • Run a weekly Lighthouse/PageSpeed check on the homepage and product pages
  • Diff the homepage against last week's version and flag unintended changes
  • Compile competitor homepage screenshots monthly for design reference
CoWork can't
  • Edit the live site without explicit per-change approval
  • Change DNS records or domain settings
  • Choose a logo or write the value prop
Setup cost: ~30 minutes once. Weekly human time: ~10 minutes.
